Company IT Security Policy — NovaGuard Technologies
1. Device use
Employees may use personal laptops for work only if 
they are enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management
 (MDM) system. Personal USB drives and external storage 
 devices are not permitted to be connected to any company 
 laptop without prior written approval from the IT Security
  team. Company-issued devices must have full-disk encryption 
  enabled at all times.

2. Password Requirements
All employee accounts must use passwords that are at least 12
characters long, containing a mix of uppercase letters, 
lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be
changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is
mandatory for all accounts with access to customer data or
 internal systems.

3. Remote Work Access
Employees working remotely must connect to internal systems 
only through the company VPN. Access from public Wi-Fi networks
(such as cafes or airports) without VPN is strictly prohibited.
Remote access requests from outside the employee's registered 
home country must be approved by a manager at least 48 hours 
in advance.

4. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including lost devices, 
phishing emails, or unauthorized access attempts, must be 
reported to the IT Security team within 4 hours of discovery. 
Reports can be made via the internal ticketing system or by 
emailing security@novaguard.example. Employees who fail to 
report a known incident within this window may be subject to 
disciplinary review.
